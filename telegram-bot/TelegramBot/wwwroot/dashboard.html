How the website is doing
Cookie-less. A visitor is a hash that changes every day; IP addresses are kept 30 days and then blanked. Your own visits (open the site once with ?internal=1, or from this server / Tailscale / WebInternalIps in Config) and bots are kept out unless you tick the box. A trial is counted when someone presses Start from a website button, and the visit it came from travels in the link, so each trial shows its country and device.
1. From visit to Telegram
Counts: Home and the guide pages · visitors (people, once per day), then Telegram accounts
2. Which page does what, and where do people go next?
Counts: every page, including Feed and Manage · visitors. “Went on” means they opened another page later in the same visit.
3. Which Telegram button gets clicked?
Counts: clicks on the “open the Telegram bot” buttons, by the page the button is on · visitors
4. Who pressed Start in Telegram?
Counts: Telegram accounts (not website visitors) that pressed Start after clicking a website button
5. Are the people we already have using the app?
Counts: the Feed and Manage pages (not the landing site) · logged-out visitors and users, as labelled
6. Where do visitors come from, and what are they using?
7. How far do they scroll?
Counts: sections reached on each page · visitors
8. Latest visits, one row per person (click to open)
