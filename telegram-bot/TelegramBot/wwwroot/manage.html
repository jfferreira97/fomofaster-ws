⚙️ Notification Settings
Repeat cooldown (min) i Silences repeat alerts for the same trader on the same token. Set it to 30 and if they buy that token three times in 30 minutes, only the first one reaches you.

Buys and sells count separately — a buy followed by a sell still sends both.
0 = alert on every trade
⛓ Chain Settings
Chain i Tap to turn that chain on or off entirely. Off means no alerts from it at all. Min MCap i Tap to set a market-cap floor for that chain — you'll only be alerted on tokens at or above it. Blank means no minimum. Trending i Tap to mute just 🔥 Trending alerts for that chain. Its normal alerts keep coming.
📊 Traders
Click a category to filter. Tap its 🔔 to get alerts from everyone in it, now and later. Or tick traders one by one; set a $ floor to skip small buys.
