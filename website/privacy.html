Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

What we collect

When you use @GROUPCHAT_ALERTS_BOT or the trader page we store only what the Service needs to work:

  • Your Telegram identity: your Telegram chat ID, username and first name, as Telegram provides them when you press Start or sign in.
  • Your settings: the traders and categories you follow, your chain and minimum-trade-size filters, and your alert preferences.
  • Subscription and payment records: when your trial and subscription start and end, and the on-chain details of the SOL payment you send to the payment address the bot gives you.
  • Website analytics: when you visit this website we record which pages you open, which Telegram button you click, the site you came from, your device, browser, system and language, and your country. The country is looked up from your IP address in a database that runs on our own server, so no third party sees your address. We keep your IP address for 30 days, to spot abuse and tell visitors apart, and then blank it. Visitors are told apart by a hash that changes every day, so nobody can be followed from one day to the next. We set no cookies for this, and we record nothing if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.
  • Technical logs: standard server logs (such as IP address and request times) kept for security and debugging.

What we do not collect

  • We never ask for your wallet's private key or seed phrase. Anyone who asks you for one is not us.
  • We do not run advertising trackers or third-party analytics on the website.
  • We do not see your trades. The Service reports other traders' public activity, not yours.

How we use it

To send you the alerts you asked for, show your follow settings, confirm your subscription, prevent abuse, and fix problems. We do not sell your personal data.

Cookies

When you sign in to the trader page we set one session cookie so you stay signed in. It is not used for advertising.

Third parties

Alerts are delivered through Telegram, which has its own privacy policy. The website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which can see your IP address when your browser fetches them. Payments are made on the Solana blockchain, which is public. Country lookups use the free IP to Country Lite database by DB-IP.com, licensed CC BY 4.0.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account is active. To ask for your data to be deleted, message @GROUPCHAT_ALERTS_BOT on Telegram. Blockchain payment records are public and cannot be erased.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Questions: message @GROUPCHAT_ALERTS_BOT on Telegram.
