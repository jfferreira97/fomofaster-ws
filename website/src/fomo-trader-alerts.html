What you get

  • Buys and sells from the FOMO traders you follow, with the size of the trade and the market cap.
  • Theses: the reason a trader gave for a buy, with the size of their bag in that token.
  • Trending alerts when five or more tracked traders buy the same coin within 30 minutes.
  • The contract and one-tap links (Axiom, Terminal, Dexscreener and more) in every alert.

Chains

FOMO activity on Solana, BNB Chain, Base, Ethereum, Monad, Robinhood Chain and Arc. You can switch chains on or off and set a minimum trade size for each.

No. It only sends alerts. You decide and trade yourself.
